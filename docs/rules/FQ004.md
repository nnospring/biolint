# FQ004: Sequence and quality have different lengths

| | |
|---|---|
| Name | `length-mismatch` |
| Format | FASTQ |
| Level | `error` |
| Source | Cock et al. 2010 (NAR 38:1767), "FASTQ definition" |

There must be exactly one quality character per base. A different count means the record is damaged: a line was cut short (truncated transfer, full disk), bases were trimmed without trimming the qualities, or two writers interleaved their output in one file. Aligners and trimmers stop on such a record.

## Example

Ten quality characters for eight bases:

```text
@read1
ACGTACGT
+
IIIIIIIIII
```

biolint reports:

```text
error[FQ004]: quality has 10 characters but the sequence of read `read1` has 8 bases
 --> example.fastq:4:9
  |
4 | IIIIIIIIII
  |         ^^
  = help: bases were removed without removing their qualities, or two records were merged; regenerate the file
  = note: record starts on line 1
  = spec: Cock et al. 2010 (NAR 38:1767), "FASTQ definition"
```

The quality of the first read is two characters short:

```text
@read1 lane 1
ACGTACGTAC
+
IIIIIIII
@read2 lane 1
ACGTACGT
+
IIIIIIII
```

biolint reports:

```text
error[FQ004]: quality has 8 characters but the sequence of read `read1` has 10 bases
 --> example.fastq:4
  = help: the record is cut short: a write was interrupted, or two writers shared the file; regenerate it and compare checksums
  = note: record starts on line 1
  = spec: Cock et al. 2010 (NAR 38:1767), "FASTQ definition"
```

In these listings `→` stands for a tab.

## Configuration

Silence it with `--ignore FQ004`, or check only this rule with `--select FQ004`.
