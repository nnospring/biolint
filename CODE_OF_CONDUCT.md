# Code of conduct

Be kind, assume good faith, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discrimination are not
tolerated in issues, pull requests or any other project space.

People arrive here because a file would not load at an inconvenient moment. Questions that seem basic are welcome.

If something is wrong, contact the maintainers through the repository's private reporting channel. Reports are handled
confidentially, and maintainers may edit or remove contributions and block participants who do not follow these rules.
